## Full description (max 4000 chars, currently ~1450)

A year of small Squares, one Square for each Day. Fill a Square by hand with
one tap. Ten seconds, once a day.

Most habit trackers put a streak on the first screen — a number that goes to
zero the moment you miss a Tuesday. squares shows a Total instead. A Day you
missed is just one empty Square in a year that keeps filling. There's no
comparison with other users, no leaderboard, no guilt screen.

Want the pressure anyway? Strict Streaks are there for any Habit you turn
them on for. Off by default.

The shape is the shape of the GitHub contribution graph. squares copies the
mechanic, not the branding — this isn't a coding-streak app, it's for
whatever daily habit you're actually building.

Keep it to two or three Habits. squares isn't built to track a dozen — the
whole point is that logging one takes ten seconds, and that stops being true
past a handful.

Everything stays on your device:

- No account, no login, no backend.
- No analytics, no ads, no tracking of any kind.
- The one daily reminder is scheduled and raised entirely on-device — no
  push service, no server, ever.
- Export your year to a .json file whenever you want a backup — the only
  copy that survives an uninstall, since clearing the app clears the record.
  Import brings it back, on this device or a new one.

squares is one screen you open once a day, not a dashboard to check on. Log
your Squares and get back to your day.
