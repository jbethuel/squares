# No backend: data stays on the device, and Share Cards are drawn on the client

All user data stays on the device and never leaves it.

Share Cards are drawn on the device as PNG files. There's no web page for a
card, so there are no accounts, no database, no privacy policy and no moderation.

The main reason is distribution, not cost. People posting Share Cards is the
only channel this app has, and putting an account screen in front of sharing
kills it. Picture finishing a 30-Day Streak, tapping share, and being asked for
your email address.

## Consequences

There's no multi-device support, and deleting the app loses the data. Export is
in v1 to soften that, and it's meant for everyone, not just power users.

If v1 does well, sync is the planned paid feature. It's the only planned feature
with an ongoing cost, and it doesn't touch the free daily use of the app.

The number of Habits won't be a paid feature. The right number is two or three,
and charging for more would be selling a limit nobody wants to cross.
