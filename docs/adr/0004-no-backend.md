# No backend: storage on the device, and Share Cards from the client

All the data stays on the device of the user. The data does not leave the
device.

The app draws the Share Card on the device and makes a PNG file. There is no web
page for a card. Thus the app has no accounts, no database, no privacy policy
and no moderation.

The main reason is distribution and not cost. Users who post a Share Card are
the only channel that this app has. An account screen in front of the share
operation stops that channel. The user completes a Streak of 30 Days, taps
share, and gets a field for an email address.

## Consequences

The app does not support more than one device. If the user deletes the app, the
data is lost. Export is in v1 to decrease this risk. Export is not a feature for
expert users only.

Sync is the planned paid feature if v1 is successful. Sync is the only planned
feature with a continuous cost, and it does not change the free daily operation.

The number of Habits is not a paid feature. The correct limit is two or three
Habits. A limit on the number sells a boundary that the user does not want to
cross.
