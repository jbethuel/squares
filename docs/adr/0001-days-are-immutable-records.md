# Days are immutable records; Habits are archived, never deleted

A Day Record permanently stores which Habits were Active that Day and which were Ticked, so a Square's Intensity is fixed once the Grace Window closes. Habits are Archived — stopped from today forward — rather than deleted.

The alternative was to treat the Heatmap as a live projection of the current Habit set. Rejected because it makes "delete the Habit I kept failing" a button that retroactively turns a patchy year green: the denominator shrinks, every past Square brightens, and the record stops being a record. That destroys the honesty of the Heatmap and the credibility of any Share Card made from it — a Chain is only worth looking at if it could not have been edited.

## Consequences

Proportion-based Intensity is unrecoverable if history lacks per-Day Active Habit sets, and it cannot be backfilled after the fact. This has to be right before any history accumulates.

Real deletion exists only as a global wipe, never as a selective rewrite of the past.
